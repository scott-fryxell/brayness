## Research checklist

Work through these using WebSearch/WebFetch. Skip rows the user already has answers for.

| Area | What to find |
|------|---------------|
| Product & business model | What they sell, who buys it, how they make money |
| Stage & funding | Public/private, funding round, headcount trend, layoffs history |
| Recent news | Last 6-12 months: launches, funding, leadership changes, controversies |
| Tech stack | From job postings, engineering blog, StackShare-style mentions |
| Competitors | Who else is in this space, how this company differentiates |
| Culture signals | Engineering blog tone, leadership podcasts/interviews, public reviews -- treat review-site scores as noise, read the actual text for patterns |
| Interviewers (if named) | Public writing/talks that hint at what they care about |

## Sourcing rules

- Prefer primary sources (company site, engineering blog, SEC/funding databases, the job posting itself) over aggregator summaries.
- Mark anything from a single unverified source as unverified in the notes rather than stating it as fact.
- Don't fabricate specifics (funding amounts, headcount, exec names) when search doesn't turn up a confident answer -- say what's unknown.
- Recent news claims need a date; don't present stale news as current.

## Output

Write findings into the company's vault note (`02 Areas/Job Hunt/Companies/<slug>.md`) under `## Research`, using the template in `assets/company-note-template.md` if the note doesn't exist yet. Keep it scannable -- bullets, not paragraphs.
